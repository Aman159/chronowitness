How to read this panel

“Elsewhere” contains only manually curated context included in this prototype. It is not a complete world history and it does not imply every entry happened on the exact same day. Ancient destinations use broad historical periods where the source does not establish a precise date.

TRUST BY DESIGN

The illusion is cinematic. The evidence is not.

✓Documented

Exact date and event are supported by an institutional source.

⌁Period-based

The source supports a broad era, so the experience avoids inventing a day.

⌖Modern geography

The map provides present-day geographic orientation, not reconstructed political boundaries.

↔Then versus now

The comparison labels clearly separate historical source material from present-day map context.

Accuracy boundary: the time tunnel, colors, motion and arrival effects are storytelling devices. They do not create evidence. All factual claims remain visible in the arrival dossier and link to the named institutional source.
